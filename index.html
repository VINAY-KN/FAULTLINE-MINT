Curator's Note & Artist Statement

FAULTLINE began from an inquiry into transformation—particularly the way mechanical and futuristic forms can alter identity as their individual parts move, connect, separate, or reorganize. The system explores modular structures that appear capable of evolving continuously beyond their original state.

Each composition is generated from a field of multi-tiered geometric plates that shift across scale, depth, orientation, color logic, and internal cell divisions. Cellular patterns, stepped elevations, layered relief, and embedded motifs synthesize architectural, geological, and extraterrestrial forms without reproducing any single archetype.

The final system is intentionally a balance between control and discovery. The artist defines the vocabulary of forms and boundaries of behavior; the on-chain seed dictates the singular reality that emerges.
