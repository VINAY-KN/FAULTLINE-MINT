About FAULTLINE

FAULTLINE began from my interest in transformation—particularly the way mechanical and futuristic forms can change identity as their individual parts move, connect, separate, or reorganize. I have always been fascinated by modular objects and structures that look as though they could keep evolving beyond their original configuration.

Each iteration is constructed from a field of geometric plates that can shift in scale, position, orientation, depth, color, and internal structure. Cellular patterns, layered surfaces, relief, embedded motifs, and intersecting forms create compositions that can feel architectural, geological, mechanical, or unfamiliar depending on the combination of elements.
